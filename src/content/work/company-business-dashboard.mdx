> Unified operations and revenue cockpit informed by hands-on experience managing daily store production and online sales in Bali.

## Overview

Running an active F&B operation across walk-in customers and online delivery channels creates a split-brain problem: sales live in one spreadsheet, kitchen batch schedules sit on paper, and fulfillment bottlenecks show up too late. I designed and built a **Company Business & Operations Dashboard** that connects executive KPIs directly to live floor execution.

## Key Engineering Decisions

- **Channel-Aware Revenue & Order Telemetry:** Separates walk-in store volume from online delivery channels across daily, weekly, and monthly windows without forcing managers to pivot raw tables.
- **Actionable Production Queue:** Integrates a real-time **Queued → In Progress → Dispatched** workflow board beside sales metrics so operations leads can clear bottlenecks from the same screen.
- **Typed Full-Stack Architecture:** Built on **Next.js**, **TypeScript**, and relational **PostgreSQL** modeling so product margins, order SLAs, and batch counts remain consistent.

## Outcome

- Replaces fragmented daily reporting with a single real-time cockpit tailored for business operators and executives.
- Combines real operational domain knowledge in Bali with production full-stack engineering.
