> Reusable AI agent skill packs that translate plain-English product briefs into deterministic 60fps motion graphics.

## Overview

When AI agents generate marketing or product clips, raw pixel-generation models often scramble typography and brand timing. Instead, I engineered **modular motion-video skills** that teach agents how to author structured, programmatic scene timelines using real spring physics and typographic hierarchy.

## Key Engineering Decisions

- **Skill-Driven Scene Grammar:** Encodes pacing, easing curves, safe margins, and color tokens as structured agent instructions and schema-validated **Timeline JSON**.
- **Deterministic 60 FPS Rendering:** Uses React-driven motion primitives paired with **FFmpeg** export pipelines so headlines, KPI counters, and charts never warp or hallucinate text.
- **Multi-Track Composition:** Separates typography, camera motion, data layers, and audio cues into clean tracks that agents can inspect, revise, and re-render on demand.

## Outcome

- Enables autonomous agents (such as **Hermes** and **OpenClaw**) to generate repeatable, brand-accurate launch promos, data stories, and social bumpers from a single brief.
- Bridges software engineering, agent tooling, and visual motion craft cleanly.
